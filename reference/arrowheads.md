# Arrowheads

A list of pre-built arrowheads

## Usage

``` r
arrowheads
```

## Format

### `arrowheads`

A list of matrices with xy coordinates

- chippenham:

  Chippenham arrowhead

- eiffel:

  Outline of the Eiffel tower

- enterprise:

  Outline of Star Trek USS Enterprise insignia

- executor:

  Outline of the Executor spacecraft from Star Wars

- flame:

  Flame-shaped arrowhead

- gateway:

  Inpsired by the St. Louis Gateway arch

- latex:

  Inspired by TikZ's latex arrowhead

- latexprime:

  Inspired by TikZ's latex' arrowhead

- leaf:

  Curved leaf arrowhead

- lozenge:

  Rhombus-shaped arrowhead

- nighthawk:

  Outline of the Lockheed F-117 Nighthawk stealth aircraft

- pantherxf70:

  Outline of the Lockheed XF-70 Panther aircraft

- saxon:

  Saxon arrowhead

- spade:

  Outline of a spade

- stardestoyer:

  Outline of a Star Destroyer spacecraft from Star Wars

- vikingleaf:

  Viking leaf arrowhead

- viper:

  Outline of the Viper spacecraft from the original television series
  Battlestar Galactica

- viper2:

  Outline of the Viper spacecraft from the rebooted television series
  Battlestar Galactica
